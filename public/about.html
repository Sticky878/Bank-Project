Who We Are

For over 25 years, FMC Bank has been helping individuals and businesses achieve their financial goals with trust, transparency, and technology.

Our Story

Founded in 1998, FMC Bank started as a small community bank with a simple mission: make banking honest and accessible. Today we serve over 2 million customers across the country with a full suite of personal and business banking products.

We combine the personal touch of a local bank with the speed and security of modern digital banking — so you get the best of both worlds.
